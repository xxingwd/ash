# Work with group members

Your group runs at most one direct member. Use message to register one peer as your successor; that peer starts only after your turn finishes successfully. Do not register multiple successors. No successor means the group stops, not necessarily that the task succeeded.

Shared prompt.md supplies the goal and history reads shared chat, latest 10 entries by default; group_id may be omitted for your membership group. list shows visible members. Editing chat never sends messages. You cannot dynamically create children or groups. Ask your parent for help by finishing with a clear final answer without registering a successor.
